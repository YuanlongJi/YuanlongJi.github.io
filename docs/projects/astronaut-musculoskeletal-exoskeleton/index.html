    Exoskeleton-Based Reproduction of Lower-Limb Musculoskeletal Biomechanical States for Astronaut Microgravity Protection

    Project Information

    • Funding Source: National Natural Science Foundation of China
    • Project Type: General Program
    • Period: Jan. 2025 – Dec. 2028
    • Status: Ongoing
    • Project No.: 52475291
    • Role: Core Research Member

    Overview

    This project investigates exoskeleton-based methods for reproducing lower-limb musculoskeletal biomechanical states under normal gravity conditions, aiming to support astronaut microgravity protection. It focuses on wearable robotic intervention, musculoskeletal biomechanics, multimodal sensing, gait phase estimation, and compliant assistive control.

    Research Scope

    • Lower-limb musculoskeletal biomechanics
    • Wearable exoskeleton systems for microgravity protection
    • Multimodal sensing and gait phase estimation
    • Human-exoskeleton interaction and compliant control
    • Human-centered assistive intervention

    My Role

    As a core research member, I am involved in wearable exoskeleton system design, mechanism innovation, human-robot interaction, and compliant control for microgravity protection applications.

    Related to my work on gait phase estimation, implicit modeling, compliant control, lower-limb exoskeletons, and multimodal sensing for wearable robotic systems.
