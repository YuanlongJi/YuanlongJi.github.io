    Research projects

    Funded projects support the mechanisms, perception, control and validation needed to bring exoskeletons into everyday settings. Funding amounts refer to total project budgets, not personal awards.

    [P1] National Natural Science Foundation of China — General Program

    2025.01–2028.12 · RMB 480,000
    Core research member

    Exoskeleton-based musculoskeletal protection for astronauts, supporting actuator design, gait-phase estimation and human–robot control.

    [P2] Beijing Natural Science Foundation — Haidian Joint Fund

    2023.01–2026.12 · RMB 200,000
    Core research member

    Rehabilitation exoskeletons and digital therapy for bone/soft-tissue healing, supporting the WRRC rehabilitation exoskeleton and reconfigurable systems.

    [P3] National Undergraduate Innovation Training Program

    Undergraduate · RMB 10,000 and 20,000 (two projects)
    Former lead / participant

    Innovation practice spanning mechanical design, prototyping and control-system integration.

    [P4] BJUT Xinghuo Key Project

    Undergraduate · RMB 3,000
    Former project lead; outstanding project

    Development of an automated takeaway sorting machine.
