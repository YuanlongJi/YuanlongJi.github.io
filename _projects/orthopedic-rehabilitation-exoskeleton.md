## Project Information

- **Funding Source:** Beijing Natural Science Foundation - Joint Fund
- **Period:** Jan. 2023 – Dec. 2026
- **Status:** Ongoing
- **Project No.:** L222139
- **Role:** Core Research Member

## Overview

This project focuses on lower-limb orthopedic perioperative rehabilitation using rehabilitation exoskeletons and digital therapy technologies. It supports personalized rehabilitation by integrating robotic modules, posture perception, human-robot interaction, and adaptive rehabilitation planning.

## Research Scope

- Orthopedic rehabilitation exoskeleton systems
- Mechanical/physical stimulation for rehabilitation
- Limb posture perception and human-robot interaction
- Multimodal sensing and rehabilitation assessment
- Adaptive rehabilitation planning and digital therapy

## My Role

As a core research member, I participated in rehabilitation exoskeleton systems, mechanism design, human-robot interaction, and rehabilitation-oriented robotic technology development.

## Related Research

Related to my work on orthopedic rehabilitation exoskeletons, coupled movable pulley mechanisms, rehabilitation robot safety control, and wearable robotic systems.
